# Product Requirements — AutoJob Intel

## Differentiator
Most job aggregators optimize for volume. AutoJob Intel optimizes for trust: official employer links, status verification, exact experience requirements, location accuracy, transparent matching, and alerts only for meaningful new opportunities.

## MVP
- Candidate preferences, resume profile, role families, radius, remote policy, seniority/stretch rules.
- Source registry for official employer career sites.
- Fetch/parse/normalize/deduplicate pipeline.
- Active-status verification with evidence and timestamp.
- Requirement extraction: experience years, education, skills, clearance, travel, schedule, location.
- Hard filters and explainable match score.
- Saved/application pipeline and resume-version recommendation.
- Email/Discord digest with deduplication and official URLs.
- Closed-job detection and notification correction.

## Quality metrics
- >95% alerted links active at send time.
- >90% extracted experience requirements correct on reviewed sample.
- Duplicate alert rate <2%.
- Every score explains hard filters, matches, gaps, and uncertainty.
